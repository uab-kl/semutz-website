Migration path

From spreadsheets and legacy point tools

Week 1 — Map what you have

We take your item list, customer list, rack layout and current price lists. Where the data disagrees with itself, we show you the conflicts before anything moves.

Week 2 — Clean master data once

Deduplicate SKUs and customers, agree pack sizes and storage rules, load the shared layer. This is the step most projects skip — it's why ours don't stall.

Week 3 — Run one process live

Usually goods-in or order capture. Your team uses the real screen on the real floor while the old method stays available in parallel.

Week 4+ — Add the next product

TMS or ERP follows on the same data. No second migration, because there is nothing to migrate — it's already the same database.

Do we have to move off our current accounting software?
No. Semutz ERP handles order-to-invoice and operations-side financial documents, then posts to your accounting ledger through a supported integration. If you already run a finance package you trust, we keep it and feed it.
What happens to our spreadsheets?
They become imports. We take them as they are, flag what can't be trusted, and give your team read-only reporting out of the system so the spreadsheet stops being the operational record.
Can we run one product without the others?
Yes. SFA or WMS can run standalone with a light master data set. The moment you add a second product, the shared layer is already in place — that's the difference from stitching point solutions together.
How is the deployment hosted?
Cloud-hosted, multi-tenant, with per-tenant data isolation and role-based access by site, warehouse, route and product. Environments are separated into production and sandbox, with audit history retained on every document.
What about devices on the floor?
Warehouse screens run on standard Android handhelds and tablets; the driver and rep apps run on Android and iOS. No proprietary hardware purchase is required to go live.

Bring your messiest data to the demo

Rack list, item master, price list — whatever state it's in. Seeing the platform work on your real data beats any slide about it.

Home/Industries

Four FMCG distribution segments. We stay in our lane.

Semutz is built for moving packaged goods from a supplier to a shelf. If your operation lives in that trail, we can help — if it doesn't, we'll tell you.

Segment 01

FMCG Manufacturers & Brands

You own the brand and increasingly own the route to market: secondary sales, van sales fleets, distributor claims and trade promotions across hundreds of outlets.

SFAWMSTMSERP
Challenges

Van stock that nobody can reconcile · trade spend approved on paper · outlet-level sell-out unknown until month end.

How Semutz helps

SFA captures every outlet order and van movement; WMS reconciles plant and DC stock; TMS proves delivery; ERP settles trade terms against real sell-in.

Expected outcomes

Outlet-level visibility inside the day · van stock variance found at the stop, not at month end · promotion spend attributable to the outlet that earned it.

Segment 02

Distributors & 3PL Warehouses

Multi-principal operations where every pallet belongs to someone else, and you bill for storage, handling and delivery on top of it.

WMSTMSERP
Challenges

Owner-level stock accuracy · handling charges rebuilt by hand each cycle · SLA claims you can't prove or disprove.

How Semutz helps

WMS keeps stock by owner, SKU, batch and bin with cycle-count evidence; TMS records every stop and exception; ERP turns activity into billable documents.

Expected outcomes

Owner stock statements generated from the same records you picked from · charges defended with timestamps · disputed delivery claims settled from POD data.

Segment 03

Retail Chains & Modern Trade

DC-to-store replenishment across many outlets with fixed delivery windows, pallet limits and store-level ranging.

WMSTMSERP
Challenges

Trucks leaving DC half-filled · stores receiving wrong pallet mixes · replenishment based on spreadsheet forecasts.

How Semutz helps

WMS builds store-specific pallet mixes at pick time; TMS sequences stops to the store's delivery window; ERP reconciles store receipts and invoices.

Expected outcomes

Higher pallet utilisation per trip · fewer store rejections at the bay · one document trail from DC release to store receipt.

Segment 04

Food & Beverage Distribution

Short shelf life, batch and expiry discipline, cold-chain handoffs and customers who will reject a pallet over three days of dating.

WMSTMSERP
Challenges

Writing off stock that rotated wrong · recalls that take days to trace · delivery windows and temperature constraints.

How Semutz helps

WMS enforces FIFO/LIFO/FEFO at allocation with batch and expiry on every movement; TMS sequences time-critical stops; ERP holds the full trace for recall.

Expected outcomes

Batch trace completed in minutes rather than by files and phone calls · less near-dated write-off · written proof of rotation policy on every order.

Banking, government, healthcare and telecom are deliberately not on this list. Semutz builds for packaged-goods distribution — that focus is the point.

Home/About

A product company built by people who ran the warehouse

Semutz isn't a consultancy that discovered supply chain. Our founding team came out of distribution operations and internal tooling — the people who used to be woken up when the pick list was wrong.

Mission

Make the trail visible

FMCG goods move through hands that rarely see the whole path. Our job is to make every movement legible — one order, one batch, one stop at a time — so the people doing the work can trust what the system tells them.

Vision

One system from supplier to shelf

A distributor should not need four vendors, three integrations and a spreadsheet to move a pallet. We're building the layer where sales, warehouse, transport and finance work on the same record by default — not as an upgrade project.

How we build

Shipping beats presenting

We sit with the operators

Product decisions get made on warehouse floors and in the cab of a truck, not in a workshop room. If a change doesn't survive a 6am shift, it doesn't ship.

We release, then listen

Short release cycles with real users on live data. Feedback arrives as a support ticket from a pick face, which is more useful than any survey.

We tell you what we don't do

No AI advisory practice, no ROI calculators, no industry-agnostic solutions language. If something is out of scope, we say so before the proposal.
